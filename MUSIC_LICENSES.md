# Star Invader Music Licenses

All current stage and boss tracks are CC0/public-domain tracks from OpenGameArt.
The soundtrack is ordered by intended game intensity: upbeat/light at Stage 1, then progressively heavier toward Stage 5.
Intensity is based on musical style and arrangement, not BPM alone.
